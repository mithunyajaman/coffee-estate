Coffee Leaf Rust

Hemileia vastatrix
Varietal Vulnerability: Highly susceptible in Arabica (Chandragiri, S.795); Kaveri Coffee (Cauvery) displays strong natural resistance.

Identified Symptoms & Field Signs:

  • Orange-yellow powdery pustules on the ventral (underside) of coffee leaves.
  • Chlorotic yellow lesions on the upper leaf surface turning necrotic brown.
  • Premature defoliation, twig die-back, and severe crop loss in subsequent seasons.
Organic / Cultural Protocol:

Apply 0.5% to 1.0% neutral Bordeaux mixture pre-monsoon (May) and post-monsoon (Oct/Nov). Spray Neem Seed Kernel Extract (NSKE 5%).

Chemical Intervention:

Copper Oxychloride (COC 50% WP @ 2.5 g/L of water) or systemic Hexaconazole 5% EC (Contaf @ 2.0 ml/L).
